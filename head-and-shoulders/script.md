## Three Peaks  ·  89 words, ~32 s

- **s011** Let's slow down and read the chart one peak at a time.
  - *Visual:* Full chart; camera zooms to the first peak.
- **s012** The first peak, at sixty, is the left shoulder. Buyers are still in control.
  - *Visual:* Blue marker 'left shoulder 60'.
- **s013** The second peak, at seventy, is the head. It is the highest point of the whole move.
  - *Visual:* Orange marker 'head 70'.
- **s014** The third peak, at sixty one, is the right shoulder. It is lower than the head. ⏸0.6s
  - *Visual:* Blue marker 'right shoulder 61'; arrow 70 → 61 labelled 'lower high'.
- **s015** That lower high is the first warning. Buyers tried again, and they ran out of strength. ⏸0.8s
  - *Visual:* Up-arrows shrinking: +18, then +9.
- **s016** The shoulders do not need to match exactly. Real charts are never that neat.
  - *Visual:* Left and right shoulder bars side by side: 60 vs 61 'close enough'.

## The Neckline  ·  95 words, ~35 s

- **s017** Now look at the two dips between the peaks. Both stopped at fifty two.
  - *Visual:* Two dots at the troughs, labelled 52.
- **s018** Join them with a line, and you have the neckline. ⏸0.6s
  - *Visual:* Yellow neckline drawn through both dips.
- **s019** Here is the key idea. Until price closes below the neckline, there is no pattern yet.
  - *Visual:* Line beyond the right shoulder fades to grey; 'not confirmed' chip.
- **s020** It is only a shape that might become one. ⏸0.6s
  - *Visual:* Ghost path that bounces off 52 and goes back up.
- **s021** When the price closes below fifty two, the pattern is confirmed. ⏸0.8s
  - *Visual:* Line closes below 52; 'confirmed' chip turns yellow.
- **s022** Volume often tells the same story. Trading is lighter on the right shoulder, then jumps on the break.
  - *Visual:* Aqua volume bars under the chart: smaller at right shoulder, tall bar at the break.
- **s023** And sometimes the price comes back up to touch the neckline from below, before it falls away. ⏸0.6s
  - *Visual:* Pullback to 53 marked 'retest'; old floor now acts as a ceiling.

## The Price Target  ·  96 words, ~35 s

- **s024** So how far might it fall? There is a simple rule of thumb.
  - *Visual:* Chart; violet ruler appears.
- **s025** Measure the height of the head above the neckline.
  - *Visual:* Vertical violet bracket from 52 to 70.
- **s026** Seventy minus fifty two is eighteen dollars. ⏸0.6s
  - *Visual:* 70 − 52 = 18 written beside the bracket.
- **s027** Now copy that same eighteen down from the neckline.
  - *Visual:* Bracket slides down below 52.
- **s028** Fifty two minus eighteen is thirty four. That is the price target. ⏸1.0s
  - *Visual:* 52 − 18 = 34; violet dashed target line at 34.
- **s029** Traders also decide where they would be wrong. A common choice is just above the right shoulder, at sixty one.
  - *Visual:* Red stop line at 61.
- **s030** Selling at the break, at fifty two, risks nine dollars to aim for eighteen.
  - *Visual:* Red bar 9 (risk) vs violet bar 18 (reward).
- **s031** That is two dollars of possible reward for every dollar of risk. ⏸1.0s
  - *Visual:* '2 : 1' badge.

## Common Mistakes  ·  81 words, ~29 s

- **s032** Three mistakes trip up beginners.
  - *Visual:* Three cards appear.
- **s033** Mistake one: acting before the neckline breaks. Many right shoulders simply turn back up.
  - *Visual:* Card 1, red: ghost path bounces off the neckline.
- **s034** Mistake two: finding the shape anywhere. It is a reversal pattern, so it needs an uptrend before it to reverse.
  - *Visual:* Card 2: same shape inside a downtrend, crossed out.
- **s035** Mistake three: treating the target as a promise. It is a rule of thumb. Price can stop short, or overshoot. ⏸0.8s
  - *Visual:* Card 3: target line with arrows above and below it.
- **s036** And a bonus: flip the chart upside down, and you get the inverse head and shoulders, a possible bottom after a fall. ⏸0.8s
  - *Visual:* Chart flips vertically; label 'inverse head and shoulders'.
